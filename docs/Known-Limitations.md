# Known limitations

- **Sleeping services** don't deliver notifications until they are opened again.
- **Some sites don't render in WebKit at all** (macOS and Linux). If a service stays blank in Safari too, Atrium can't show it either.

The limitations that come from one platform's web view or desktop are listed on its page: [macOS](macOS#known-limitations) (among them passkeys), [Linux](Linux#known-limitations), [Windows](Windows#known-limitations).
